Correction

An earlier version of this page put PwrAgent's masthead band at 44 pt, taken from the comments in app.css. Measured in a render harness it was 38: those comments describe a 34 pt icon button that is now 28. At 38, a stoplight at y=18 sat with 18 pt above it and 6 below — 6 pt low, not 3.

B · the real defect · sidebar masthead at 3×

The stoplights were the symptom. The band was the bug.

Orange = the chrome band. Green = its centreline, and the 16 pt sidebar rail. Every bar in the app padded its content down from the top instead of centring it in the band.

Root cause

Four bars declared min-height: 44px with padding-top: 10px and no bottom padding, all commented “10px + the 34px icon button.” .sidebar__icon-button was later tightened from 34 to 28 and nothing was re-derived. The bands kept their old height, the content kept all its slack above, and each bar drifted below its own centre by a different amount.

The band

--chrome-band-h: 40px. The 28 pt button already puts a 6 pt ring around a 16 pt icon; the band gives the button row the same 6 pt ring, so 28 + 6 + 6 = 40 and the row nests concentrically. Everything centres in it — including the stoplights, at (40 − 14)/2 = 13.

What this was doing to the Settings window

Its left nav brand sat at 27 and its right-pane breadcrumb at 19.5 — 7.5 pt apart, on one screen, side by side. That is the same root cause showing up as a visible misalignment rather than as a general sense of things sitting low.

Guard

macos-window-chrome.test.ts now walks all five selectors and fails if any one drops min-height: var(--chrome-band-h) or re-introduces a non-zero top padding — the two moves that let a bar off the centreline. It also pins the stoplight y to half the band, so the buttons can never drift away from the content again.

D · the change

One token, five bars, one constant

app.css · :root
+ --chrome-band-h: 40px;
app.css · each of the five bars
- min-height: 44px; padding-top: 10px;
+ min-height: var(--chrome-band-h); padding-top: 0;
macos-window-chrome.ts
- trafficLightPosition: { x: 20, y: 18 },
+ trafficLightPosition: { x: 16, y: 13 },

The stoplight y follows the band rather than leading it: 16 is the sidebar rail, 13 is half of (40 − 14). Both numbers are now readable off the CSS, and a test says so.

Not changing

The wordmark stays at x = 96 and the 80 pt stoplight reservations stay as they are — the button group ends at 76, so they still clear it. PwrGit's 12 / 10 is correct for its 32 pt bar. PwrSnap's y=18 is correct for its 52 pt bar; only its x is off, and that is a separate repo.
