Money is mono, prose is not

Every figure on this surface — dollars, token counts, timestamps, durations — renders in IBM Plex Mono so columns of them align. The labels beside them stay in Geist. That split is the rail's whole typographic rule.

Savings, stated as three terms

The Token Miser headline is a derived figure, and the card shows its arithmetic rather than asserting it. Savings is term 1 minus terms 2 and 3.

Terms are absent until a gate is priced

A gate is priced only once its own usage line lands and the parent turn has a known model and rate, so a live turn legitimately shows decisions with no dollar terms. The card degrades to a bare decision count rather than showing a zero.

Gate rows nest under their turn

A gate is created mid-turn, after the turn's first usage flush, so in a flat time-sorted list every gate sorts above the turn that produced it. Each is a full card, so a turn with 25 gates pushed its own row a screen below its own noise. They are partitioned under the anchoring turn instead.

Where the toggles live

Settings › Usage & Pricing owns display options (codexCredits, usd) and the pricing catalogue. Per-thread Token Miser is overridden from the composer's thread menu, not from Settings — gating adds a synchronous helper round trip per large tool result, so a latency-sensitive thread needs to opt out without leaving the thread.
